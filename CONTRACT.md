# Contract between the phone app, the Google Sheet and the desktop app

Three parts talk through one Apps Script web app (`apps-script/Code.gs`) over a Google Sheet:

- the **phone app** (`app/`, this repository) sends the start and the end of each trip;
- the **desktop app** ([control-vehicles](https://github.com/alexwarrior1991/control-vehicles), `MobileClient`)
  publishes drivers and vehicles and pulls the finished trips.

A change here is made in the same change on the other side. Fields are only **added**; renaming or removing one
breaks the other part.

## Transport

- `GET <web app URL>?action=…&…` and `POST <web app URL>` with a JSON body. The phone sends POST bodies as
  `text/plain;charset=utf-8` so that the browser does not send a CORS preflight; the script reads the body
  whatever its type.
- Apps Script answers `302` to `script.googleusercontent.com`, which serves the JSON: follow the redirect with
  a `GET` (browsers and Java's `HttpClient` do).
- Every answer is HTTP 200 with JSON: `{"ok": true, …}` or `{"ok": false, "error": "…"}`. Errors:
  `Unknown link`, `Unknown vehicle`, `Unknown trip`, `Missing trip id`, `Wrong admin secret`, `Unknown action`,
  `Run setup first: …`, or the text of an unexpected exception.
- Dates are the phone's **local** time without zone, `yyyy-MM-ddTHH:mm` (as the driver lives it). Everything
  stored in the Sheet is text.

## Phone → Sheet (authenticated by the driver's token, `token`)

| Call | Body / parameters | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| `GET action=config` | `token` | `driver {id, name}`, `vehicles [{id, plate, label, lastKm}]`, `openTrip {id, vehicleId, plate, startAt, startKm}` or `null` |
| `POST start` | `token, id, vehicleId, at, km, lat, lng, accuracy` | `{id}`; the same `id` again → `{id, duplicate: true}` and nothing changes |
| `POST end` | `token, id, at, km?, tolls?, lat, lng, accuracy` | `{id}`; a trip already ended → `duplicate: true` |
| `POST cancel` | `token, id` | `{id}` (status `CANCELLED`) |

`id` is generated by the phone (UUID) so resending is safe. `lat`, `lng`, `accuracy` (metres) are `null` without
a GPS fix. `km` is a whole number; `end.km` and `tolls` (euros, `.` decimal) may be `null`. A driver can only end
or cancel their own trips.

## Desktop → Sheet (authenticated by `secret`, the `ADMIN_SECRET` of the script properties)

| Call | Body / parameters | Answer |
|---|---|---|
| `POST publish` | `secret, drivers [{token, driverId, name}], vehicles [{vehicleId, plate, label, lastKm}]` | `{drivers, vehicles}` counts; replaces both lists (a token no longer listed stops working) |
| `GET action=export` | `secret` | `trips [...]`: every row not yet acknowledged, all fields as text (below) |
| `POST ack` | `secret, ids [...]` | `{acknowledged}`: those trips are not exported again |

Exported trip fields (columns of the `Trips` tab): `id, driverId, driverName, vehicleId, plate, startAt,
startLat, startLng, startAccuracy, startKm, endAt, endLat, endLng, endAccuracy, endKm, tolls, status
(OPEN | CLOSED | CANCELLED), imported, updatedAt`. The desktop imports `CLOSED` trips and acknowledges them and
the `CANCELLED` ones; `OPEN` trips wait for their end.
